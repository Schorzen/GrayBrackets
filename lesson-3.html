Headings and paragraphs

Headings tell visitors (and search engines like Google) what each part of your page is about. HTML has six levels, from <h1> for the main title down to <h6> for the smallest. Normal text goes in paragraphs, using <p>.

Think of a newspaper: the big headline is your <h1>, section titles are <h2>, and the stories underneath are paragraphs.

Inside a paragraph you can mark important words with <strong> (usually shown in bold) and emphasized words with <em> (usually shown in italics).

<h1>Aling Nena's Store</h1>
<h2>Today's specials</h2>
<p>Fresh <strong>pandesal</strong> every morning.</p>
<h2>Opening hours</h2>
<p>Open <em>every day</em>, 6 AM to 9 PM.</p>
Tip: Isang <h1> lang bawat page. Use it once, for the main title, then go in order: h2, h3, and so on. Don't pick a heading just because of its size; changing size is CSS's job.
