Final exam and project

You've reached the end of HTML & CSS Foundations. To earn your free certificate, you'll do two things:

  1. Pass the final exam with a score of 80% or higher. You can retake it after reviewing the lessons.
  2. Build and submit your project (brief below).
Coming soon. The exam opens once free student accounts are ready, so your score and certificate are saved to your name. You can start the project now.

Project brief: a one-page site for a local business

Pick a real or made-up local business, like a sari-sari store, carinderia, barbershop or tutoring service, and build its home page. Your page must include:

  • A complete HTML page with doctype, head, title and body (lesson 2)
  • One <h1> and at least two <h2> sections (lesson 3)
  • At least one link and one image with good alt text (lesson 4)
  • A list and a price table (lesson 5)
  • CSS that sets colors, font sizes and spacing, using at least one class (lesson 6)

When it's ready, put it on GitHub Pages, the same way this site is hosted, and keep the link. You'll submit the live link and your GitHub link with the exam.
