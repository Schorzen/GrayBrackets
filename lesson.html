Headings and paragraphs

Headings tell visitors (and search engines) what each part of your page is about. HTML has six levels, from <h1> for the main title down to <h6> for the smallest. Paragraphs of normal text use <p>.

Think of a newspaper: the big headline is your <h1>, section titles are <h2>, and the stories underneath are paragraphs.

Tip: Isang <h1> lang bawat page. Use it once, for the main title, then go in order: h2, h3, and so on. Don't skip levels just to make text smaller; that's what CSS is for.
